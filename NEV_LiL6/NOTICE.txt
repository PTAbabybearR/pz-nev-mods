PROJECT ZOMBOID
Project Zomboid is (c) The Indie Stone. These mods are made for the game and
reuse some of the game's own content. Those parts remain The Indie Stone's;
they are not licensed under the Mozilla Public License or LICENSE-ART.txt:
  - The vehicle scripts (NEV_LiL6: nev_lil6.txt; NEV_Cybertruck:
    nev_cybertruck.txt) follow the structure of the game's own vehicle
    scripts and reuse parts of their templates and values (about 85-90 lines
    per vehicle), among them the install / uninstall rules of
    vehicle_suv_template.txt, the glove box of template_glovebox.txt and,
    for the Li Auto L6, collision boxes derived from
    template_suv_collision.txt. This is how vehicle mods are made for the
    game.
  - The repair recipes "Fix LiL6 Trunk Welding" (NEV_LiL6:
    nev_lil6_fixing.txt) and "Fix NEV Roof Rack Welding" (NEVLib:
    nev_fixing.txt) are the game's "Fix Trunk Welding" (vehiclesfixing.txt)
    for other items.
  - The frunk template (NEVLib: nev_frunk.txt) is modelled on the game's
    trunk part (template_trunk.txt, TruckBed).
  - The infotainment screen item (NEVLib: nev_items.txt, InfotainmentScreen)
    copies the device properties of the game's TvBlack.
  - The fridge factors (NEVLib: Fridge.lua, FRIDGE_FACTOR and ROT_SPEED) are
    the values of the game's Food.getFridgeFactor / Food.getFoodRotSpeed.
No other game asset (model, texture, sound, decompiled code) is included.
Where the mods show game images or models (for example vanilla icons), they
load them from the game.

僵尸毁灭工程
《僵尸毁灭工程》（Project Zomboid）版权属于 The Indie Stone。本系列 mod 是为
这款游戏做的，复用了游戏本身的一部分内容。这些部分仍归 The Indie Stone 所有，
不按 Mozilla Public License 或 LICENSE-ART.txt 授权：
  - 车辆脚本（NEV_LiL6 的 nev_lil6.txt、NEV_Cybertruck 的 nev_cybertruck.txt）
    沿用游戏原版车辆脚本的结构，并复用了其中部分模板和数值（每台车约 85–90 行），
    包括 vehicle_suv_template.txt 的拆装规则、template_glovebox.txt 的手套箱，
    以及理想L6 由 template_suv_collision.txt 换算出的碰撞箱。这是给本游戏做车辆
    mod 的通常做法。
  - 维修配方「Fix LiL6 Trunk Welding」（NEV_LiL6 的 nev_lil6_fixing.txt）和
    「Fix NEV Roof Rack Welding」（NEVLib 的 nev_fixing.txt）是游戏原版的
    「Fix Trunk Welding」（vehiclesfixing.txt）换成了别的物品。
  - 前备箱模板（NEVLib 的 nev_frunk.txt）照游戏原版的后备箱部件
    （template_trunk.txt 的 TruckBed）写成。
  - 中控屏物品（NEVLib 的 nev_items.txt 里的 InfotainmentScreen）照抄了游戏原版
    TvBlack 的设备属性。
  - 冰箱系数（NEVLib 的 Fridge.lua 里的 FRIDGE_FACTOR、ROT_SPEED）是游戏原版
    Food.getFridgeFactor / Food.getFoodRotSpeed 里的数值。
除此之外不包含游戏的任何其他资源（模型、贴图、声音、反编译代码）。mod 里显示的
游戏图片或模型（例如原版图标）是从游戏本身加载的。
